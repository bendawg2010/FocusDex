147 creatures across 13 families.

Every creature spawns under different conditions — the time, the app, the weather, the streak. Some only appear during a real solar eclipse. Some are locked to your birthday. Some only spawn in Tokyo.

Heads up: Only the three starters have sprite art so far. The rest are silhouette placeholders — more art coming as Claude Design generates it.
