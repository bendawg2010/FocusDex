The Loop

Focus → Spawn → Catch → Brag. That's the whole game.

1

Focus

Pick a duration. Hit start. Notchy starts your session. Every 5 minutes of focus = one Pokéball. Longer sessions earn rarer ball tiers.

2

Spawn

Creatures spawn quietly during your session — shaped by time of day, the app you're in, the weather, your streak. Some only appear during a real eclipse.

3

Catch

Timer ends → Safari Mode opens. Spawned creatures float on screen. Tap → ball arcs → wobble x3 → ✨ caught ✨.

4

Brag

Battle friends. Trade share codes. Co-op raids. Year-end Wrapped. Your Pokédex is your year of work.

Heads up — read this first.

Free + open source · MIT licensed. macOS will say "FocusDex can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software" the first time. That's because Apple charges $99/year for a Developer ID and this app is free. It's safe — every line of code is on GitHub.

  1. Open the DMG → drag FocusDex.app to your Applications folder.
  2. Launch it once → macOS will pop the warning → click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → click Open Anyway.
  4. Re-launch FocusDex → click Open on the prompt → 🎉 you're in.

You only do this once. Future opens are normal.
