The Core Loop

It's stupidly simple. Focus → earn balls → catch on break.

1

Focus

Hit start. Notchy watches you work. Every 5 minutes of uninterrupted focus = 1 Pokeball. 25 min = a Great Ball. 90 min = an Ultra Ball. 8h day = a Master Ball.

2

Spawn

While you focus, creatures appear in the world around you — shaped by time of day, which app you're in, the weather, even your streak. Some only appear during a real eclipse.

3

Catch

Break time. Open Safari Mode. Your stockpile of balls. The creatures that spawned. Go. Long focus = better odds. Catch them, name them, level them.

4

Battle. Trade. Brag.

Friday Tournaments. Co-op Raids. Trade share codes. Become a Gym Leader. Year-end Wrapped. Your Pokédex IS your year of work.
